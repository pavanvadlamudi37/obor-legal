Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Twisted Game ("we", "us") handles information when you use One Bullet One Room (the "Game"). This is the single, current version of our Privacy Policy and supersedes any other privacy policy page previously published for this Game.

Information the Game stores on your device

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including settings, high scores, stage progress, selected themes, and a player display name if you choose to enter one. This information helps the Game remember your progress and preferences, and is not transmitted anywhere unless you enable the online leaderboard described below.

Online leaderboard and restore data

If you enable online features, the Game sends your chosen display name, player code, scores, stage progress, and timestamps to Firebase Realtime Database (a Google service) so that leaderboards and game-progress restoration can work. This information may be visible to other players on the public leaderboard. Please do not use your real name, email address, phone number, or other sensitive personal information as your display name or player code.

Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address (which may be used to derive an approximate location), device information, and ad-interaction data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

Where required by your region (including the EEA, UK, and other jurisdictions with similar requirements), the Game shows a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform the first time you open it, so you can choose whether ads are personalized. You can review or change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the Game.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics to collect app activity, device, and diagnostic information, which helps us understand how the Game is used and improve performance and stability.

How information is used

We use the information described above to run the Game, save progress, provide leaderboard and restore features, deliver and measure advertising, maintain security, and improve the Game. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to those described in this policy.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to operate these features Google Firebase and Google AdMob and when required by law. Public leaderboard information (display name, score) may be visible to other players.

Data retention and deletion

Device-local data remains on your device until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall the Game. Online leaderboard and restore information is retained for as long as the related feature is available.

To request deletion of your online leaderboard or restore data, email us at pavanvadlamudi37@gmail.com with your player code or display name and the approximate date of your last score submission. We will action verified deletion requests within 30 days.

Your privacy choices and rights

  • Ad personalization: manage anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the Game.
  • Leaderboard participation: entirely optional the Game is fully playable offline without it.
  • Access, correction, or deletion of online data: contact us using the details below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws (e.g. the CCPA), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and the right to object to certain processing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in the sense used by these laws beyond the standard Google AdMob advertising described above, which you can opt out of as described here.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us at the email below and we will take steps to review and remove it.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The latest version will always be posted at this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact Twisted Game (Pavan Vadlamudi) at pavanvadlamudi37@gmail.com, or via the developer's GitHub profile.
