Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Twisted Game handles information when you use One Bullet One Room (the “Game”).

Information the Game stores

The Game stores gameplay information on your device, including settings, high scores, stage progress, selected themes, and a player display name if you choose to enter one. This information helps the Game remember your progress and preferences.

Online leaderboard and restore data

If online features are enabled, the Game may send your chosen display name, player code, scores, stage progress, and timestamps to Firebase Realtime Database so that leaderboards and game-progress restoration can work. Do not use your real name, email address, phone number, or other sensitive personal information as your display name or player code.

Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad-interaction data to provide, measure, and improve ads. Where required, the Game uses Google’s consent tools to request and manage consent. You can access available ad privacy options from the Game settings.

Analytics

The Game uses Firebase Analytics. Firebase Analytics may collect app activity, device, and diagnostic information to help us understand game performance and improve the experience.

How information is used

We use the information described above to run the Game, save progress, provide leaderboards and restore features, deliver and measure advertising, maintain security, and improve the Game. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate these features, including Google Firebase and Google AdMob, and when required by law. Public leaderboard information may be visible to other players.

Data retention and deletion

Device-local data remains until you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall the Game. Online leaderboard and restore information may be retained while the relevant feature is available. To request deletion of online information associated with a player code, contact Twisted Game using the link below and include the relevant player code.

Children

The Game is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so we can review the request.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Twisted Game through the developer’s GitHub profile.
