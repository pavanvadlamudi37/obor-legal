# Privacy Policy for One Bullet One Room

**Effective date:** 17-Aug-2026

This privacy policy explains how "One Bullet One Room" ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

## Summary

The app is a single-player arcade game. It does **not** require an account or login, does **not** collect your name, email, phone number, or location, and does **not** access your camera, microphone, or contacts. It uses two third-party services — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Firebase (leaderboard) — described below.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Advertising data (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads) through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob and its partners may collect:
- Advertising ID / device identifiers
- General device information (device model, OS version, language, screen size)
- Approximate location derived from IP address
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)

This data is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners, not directly by us. Depending on your region (EEA/UK and others), the app shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it, letting you choose whether ads are personalized. You can change this choice at any time from **Settings → Ad Privacy Options** inside the app.

Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner sites/apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### 2. Leaderboard data (Google Firebase Realtime Database)
If you choose to enter a display name and submit a score to the global leaderboard, the app stores:
- The name you typed (up to 16 characters — please do not enter real personal information)
- Your score
- A randomly generated device identifier (not linked to your real identity, used only so each device has one leaderboard row instead of duplicates)
- The date/time of submission

This data is stored in a Firebase Realtime Database operated by us on Google's infrastructure and is visible to other players on the public leaderboard. If you don't use the leaderboard feature, none of this is collected — scores stay only on your device.

### 3. Data stored only on your device
Your high score, stage progress, and settings (music/SFX volume, control scheme, speed) are saved locally on your device using browser local storage. This data is never transmitted anywhere and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

## Children's Privacy
The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

## Your Choices
- **Ad personalization:** change anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the app.
- **Leaderboard:** entering a name and submitting a score is optional; you can play without it.
- **Data deletion:** uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. To request removal of a leaderboard entry, contact us with your device ID (shown in-app) or approximate submission time.

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with:
- **Google AdMob** — to serve and measure ads (see above)
- **Google Firebase** — to host the optional leaderboard

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Effective date" above will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
